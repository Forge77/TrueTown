    Officer accountability

    Laws of TrueTown

    LAW 01 / JEJU ISLAND ONLY

    Respect the traffic rules

    Keep to your lane, respect the speed limit, and give way at STOP signs. An officer may stop you and issue a warning if you are driving dangerously or endangering other drivers.

    LAW 02 / ROAD COURTESY

    Respect others on the road

    Pay attention to other drivers. Give large hauls room, and if another driver is travelling faster, wait for a safe opportunity rather than risking a crash to pass. An officer may stop and warn you, and reduce your karma, if a deliberate action puts another driver in potential danger.

    REPUTATION / REWARDS

    TrueKarma

    TrueTown uses karma to recognise how you treat the community. Good deeds raise your standing and unlock passive cash bonuses. Negative standing does not deduct cash; it restricts access to selected licenses, vehicles and roles. Take care of the town, and the town will take care of you. Everyone starts at 0, with a score from -1000 to +1000.

    Negative standing

    Careless Driver-1 to -199 · 0% cash bonus
    Reckless Driver-200 to -499 · 0% cash bonus
    Public Hazard-500 to -749 · 0% cash bonus
    Unfit to Drive-750 to -1000 · 0% cash bonus
    • -5 karma when tagged by police
    • -15 karma for a further recorded offence after apprehension
    • -20 karma for five SAR protocol warnings

    Positive standing

    Steady Driver0 to 199 · 0% cash bonus
    Reliable Driver200 to 499 · +10% cash bonus
    Trusted Operator500 to 749 · +20% cash bonus
    True Driver750 to 1000 · +30% cash bonus
    • +20 karma for earning a license
    • +10 karma for a SAR assist; +30 when below zero
    • Towing, escorts and player assistance can build standing

    PASSIVE PAYOUTS

    Bonuses that stack

    PER JOB

    Cash bonuses are calculated from the base payout of each completed job. Your TrueKarma tier bonus stacks with every applicable license bonus and each additional trailer.

    STEADY DRIVER+0%
    RELIABLE DRIVER+10%
    TRUSTED OPERATOR+20%
    TRUE DRIVER+30%
    01
    +10% per applicable license

    Every applicable license adds +10% to the base payout for a job it authorises, including Category C Heavy Goods Vehicle, Category CE Articulated HGV, Abnormal Load Escort and other earned licenses.

    02
    +10% per additional trailer

    Each trailer in the combination adds another 10%. Trailer bonuses stack with the relevant license bonuses.

    03
    Calculated from the job payout

    These are payout bonuses, not a flat cash amount. All applicable percentages add together before the job bonus is paid.

    STACKING EXAMPLESemi rig + 3 trailers = +40% haulage bonus

    Category CE Articulated HGV contributes +10%; three additional trailers contribute +30%. Add every other applicable license bonus and the driver's TrueKarma tier on top.

    Access restrictions

    Negative karma does not impose a direct cash penalty. Access restrictions become stricter as standing falls.

    01 / CARELESS DRIVER

    Police applications closed

    Careless Driver and every lower tier cannot apply for a Police role.

    02 / RECKLESS DRIVER

    Special-load access removed

    Reckless Driver and below lose access to Special Types Heavy Combination and Abnormal Load Escort.

    03 / PUBLIC HAZARD

    No semi-rig access

    Public Hazard and below lose access to semi rigs and their components, including Category CE Articulated HGV, ADR Tanker, Load Security, Double-Trailer, Triple-Trailer and Multi-trailer Heavy Combination.

    04 / UNFIT TO DRIVE

    All license access restricted

    Unfit to Drive loses access to all licenses, including the Category C Heavy Goods Vehicle Certificate.

    How karma works

    Recorded actions

    Karma updates after verified events such as licenses, assistance, enforcement and SAR protocol.

    Positive bonuses

    Passive tier bonuses apply to job cash payouts: 0%, 10%, 20% or 30%.

    Recovery

    Karma does not decay passively. Gains come from positive actions and count double while your score is below zero.

    Access, not fines

    Negative tiers do not subtract cash. Restrictions apply to role applications, vehicle classes and licenses. SAR remains open at the lower tiers unless a separate protocol restriction applies.

    Fair review

    Each change is logged and can be appealed. Only server-verified events count in the live system.

    TrueTown should be a place where people can relax, enjoy the game and feel welcome. These community standards are managed by the Management & Administration Team, not by Police officers acting in-game.

    RULE 01

    Respect everyone

    No bullying, harassment, threats, targeted humiliation or discrimination. Treat people with dignity, including during disagreements.

    Possible consequence: Formal warning, temporary mute or suspension depending on severity and repetition.

    RULE 02

    Keep it civil

    Debate ideas without personal attacks. Do not provoke, bait or repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop. Take heated disputes to a private support channel.

    Possible consequence: Warning, chat restrictions or temporary suspension.

    RULE 03

    No hate or discriminatory conduct

    Slurs, hateful conduct and demeaning people over personal characteristics are not acceptable, whether directed at a player or a group.

    Possible consequence: Immediate escalation to an Administration review; serious incidents may result in removal.

    RULE 04

    Play honestly and do not disrupt the community

    No scams, impersonation, malicious misinformation, exploit abuse, fabricated reports or deliberate attempts to disrupt community spaces.

    Possible consequence: Warning, removal of affected privileges or suspension, based on evidence and impact.

    RULE 05

    Respect boundaries and staff processes

    Do not publish private information, encourage dogpiling, or retaliate against someone for making a good-faith report. Raise concerns through the appropriate support or appeal channel.

    Possible consequence: Content removal, restricted access or suspension for retaliation or serious privacy violations.

    Management & Administration reviews reports, considers context and evidence, and determines proportionate action. In-game traffic enforcement and role accountability are covered separately under TrueLaw.
